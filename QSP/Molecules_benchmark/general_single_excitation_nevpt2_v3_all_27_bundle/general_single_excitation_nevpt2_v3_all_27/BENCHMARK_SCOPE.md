## Conservatively excluded at the molecule level

The following molecules each have at least one state explicitly described or
flagged as pure, significant, or mixed double-excitation character in the
benchmark discussion/tables:

- Carbon trimer
- Nitrosomethane
- Acrolein
- Butadiene
- Glyoxal
- Cyclopentadiene
- Benzene
- Tetrazine

Many states of those excluded molecules remain predominantly singly excited;
they are omitted only to preserve the conservative molecule-level scope used in
this workflow.
